Help & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding our scaffolding rental terms, delivery locations, and trade options.

What is the minimum rental tenure for scaffolding?

We provide flexible rental plans ranging from daily basis for short repair/painting works up to multi-month and annual contracts for massive infrastructure and high-rise building projects.

Which locations do you supply scaffolding to?

Our main yard is centrally located at Rampur, Firozabad, Uttar Pradesh. We supply across Firozabad, Agra, Mathura, Aligarh, Kanpur, Lucknow, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, and throughout North India.

Can we buy scaffolding materials permanently instead of renting?

Yes! As highlighted in our brand services ("Scaffolding on Rent & Trade"), we offer direct sales of both brand new and certified refurbished scaffolding pipes, cuplock sets, props, and couplers with buyback options.

How quickly can you deliver material to our construction site?

For standard cuplock, props, and pipe requirements, we dispatch directly from our stock within 12 to 24 hours of agreement finalization. Emergency same-day supplies are also accommodated.

How do we calculate how much scaffolding material we need?

You can use our interactive Online Rental Estimator above, or simply WhatsApp your architectural elevations / built-up drawings to +91 9275294224. Our engineering team will prepare a precise bill of quantities (BOQ) free of charge!
